# Building

This section covers the physical construction of the FTC robot — from CAD design to fabrication, assembly, and wiring.

> **This section is under development.** Content will be added as the team documents its building processes and best practices.

- **Chassis & Drivetrain Assembly** — mounting motors, wheels, and structural components
- **Wiring** — connecting motors, servos, and sensors to the Control Hub
- **Mechanisms** — intake, lift, and scoring subsystems
- **Maintenance** — pre-match checklists, common repairs, and spare parts
