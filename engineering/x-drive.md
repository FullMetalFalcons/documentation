## Design Considerations

- **Mounting angle** — wheels must be precisely at 45° to the chassis; small errors compound into drift
- **Ground clearance** — the 45° mounting can reduce effective ground clearance depending on wheel size
- **When to use X-Drive** — consider it when your chassis geometry benefits from diagonal wheel placement, or when you want slightly different strafing characteristics than mecanum
